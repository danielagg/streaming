# Whiskey Sip image-generation prompts

Mode used: built-in image editing plus local chroma-key removal.

Authoritative character reference:

`Berry/PNGTuber_Remix_Rig/previews/preview_idle_transparent.png`

All four source poses preserve the olive-green frog, cream belly, green spots, curled brown moustache, charcoal beret and green pom-pom, painterly cartoon linework, full-body composition, and viewer-right hand-on-hip pose. The viewer-left arm holds a stylized pale-gray tumbler with dark amber whiskey and one ice cube.

Every generation used a flat `#ff00ff` field with no shadow, gradient, texture, text, or watermark. The glass was intentionally drawn mostly opaque with strong highlights and a dark outline so chroma-key removal would remain clean.

## Pose prompts

1. **Lift:** drinking arm gently bent; tumbler at lower-chest height; neutral open eyes.
2. **Approach:** tumbler raised upright to just below the moustache; elbow more tightly bent; neutral open eyes.
3. **Sip:** glass tilted toward the mouth, liquid surface tilted consistently, relaxed closed eyes, moustache retained around the rim.
4. **Satisfied:** glass upright at upper-chest height, slightly reduced pour, half-lidded pleased expression.

For each edit, the canvas, framing, identity, style, palette, proportions, hat, body, legs, non-drinking arm, and background were explicitly constrained to remain unchanged.
