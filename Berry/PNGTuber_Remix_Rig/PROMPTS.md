# Image-generation prompt set

Mode used: built-in image editing.

Authoritative edit target/reference: `Berry/Draft.png`.

Every prompt preserved the frog character's olive-green body, dark curled moustache, charcoal beret with green pom-pom, cream belly, spots, painterly cartoon linework, full-body pose, and two nostril dots. All generated sources used a flat `#ff00ff` chroma-key field with no shadow, gradient, texture, text, or watermark.

## Neutral underpaint

Isolate the large full-body character from the left of the reference sheet. Remove both eyes and their rims, the entire moustache, and the tiny mouth mark. Repaint those regions as continuous matching green face and cream upper-belly surfaces, with no holes, sockets, residual shadows, or facial-feature fragments. Keep the nostril dots and all non-face design details unchanged.

## Eye states

Starting from the neutral underpaint, add only the paired eye artwork:

1. **Open:** glossy black frog eyes with warm brown reflections, white highlights, olive rims, asymmetric sizing, and forward gaze.
2. **Half:** an early synchronized descending blink; pupils and highlights are occluded rather than vertically squashed.
3. **Three-quarter:** a deeper transition with only a small lower portion of each iris still visible.
4. **Closed:** relaxed dark curved lid strokes with matching green lids; no iris, pupil, sclera, or highlight remains.

The mouth/moustache region stays blank in every eye source.

## Mouth + moustache states

Starting from the neutral underpaint, add only a seam-proof combined mouth-and-moustache pose. Keep the moustache's bridge/root anchored and preserve its brown texture, outline weight, and curled identity:

1. **Closed/silent:** calm resting moustache covering a tiny closed mouth seam.
2. **Narrow/teeth:** shallow horizontal opening with a thin upper-teeth band; moustache center lifts slightly.
3. **Wide E/I:** wider medium-height opening with upper teeth and modest tongue/lower lip; moustache halves spread and lift.
4. **Round O/R/U:** compact vertical O opening; moustache splits/lifts around the fixed center.
5. **Open A/AH/G/K:** deep rounded opening with tongue and optional upper-teeth edge; moustache lifts/spreads the most.

The eye regions stay blank in every mouth source. Each prompt requires unchanged canvas, placement, body, hat, shading, and background.
